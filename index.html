Who we are

Exoctic International ("we", "us") provides connected business software for billing, payroll and HR, travel management and reporting, and builds custom trip apps for iOS, Android and the web. This policy explains how we handle personal data on this website and in our services.

For questions about this policy, email .

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the website and deliver our services:

  • Contact details you send us through the demo or newsletter forms: name, work email, company, team size, products you are interested in and any message you write.
  • Customer data in the platform when your company becomes a client: customers, invoices, employees, attendance, payroll, trips, bookings and expenses. Your company controls this data and we process it on your instructions.
  • Trip app data from travellers using an app we build for you, such as itineraries, documents and receipts they upload.
  • Technical data such as browser type and pages requested, kept in standard server logs for security.

How we use it

  • To reply to demo requests and send the quotes and calendar links you ask for.
  • To send the monthly product update, only if you subscribe. Every email has a way to unsubscribe.
  • To provide, secure, support and improve the platform and trip apps.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use customer data to advertise.

Legal bases

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (newsletter), steps taken at your request before a contract (demo requests), performance of our contract with your company, our legitimate interest in running a secure service, and compliance with legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with providers that help us run the service, under written agreements that limit their use of it:

  • Hosting and backup providers in the EU or US region your company chooses.
  • Our form-processing provider, which delivers website form submissions to our inbox.
  • Payment providers your company connects, such as Stripe or PayPal, for invoices and collections.
  • App store operators (Apple and Google) when we publish a trip app under your developer accounts.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

International transfers

Customer data stays in the hosting region your company selects. Where data moves between countries, we use safeguards recognised by the applicable law, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Demo requests are kept for up to 24 months after our last contact. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. Customer data is kept for the length of the contract, then exported to you on request and deleted within 30 days, except where the law requires longer retention. Audit logs are kept for seven years.

Security

Data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2 or later in transit, including backups. Access is role-based, protected by two-factor sign-in, and logged. Backups run daily with point-in-time recovery.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time. If your data is held in the platform by your employer or travel provider, please contact them first; we will help them respond. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. The platform uses essential cookies to keep you signed in.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children under 16. Trip apps may hold details of minors travelling in a group only when the client company provides them.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell clients by email before it takes effect.
